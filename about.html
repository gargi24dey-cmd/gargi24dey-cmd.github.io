About Me

I’m an MSc Economics student at Symbiosis School of Economics, Pune, with a focus on data analytics. I completed my BSc. in Economics (Hons) from the University of Calcutta, where I built a strong foundation in economics, statistics, and mathematics.

What interests me most is using data to understand real-world economic and business problems and turn complex information into meaningful insights. Through my academic and personal projects, I’ve worked with tools such as Python, SQL, R, Power BI, Excel, and Stata across areas including retail sales, airline pricing, socioeconomic development, and macroeconomic trends.

Outside academics, I’ve also explored my creative side by running a small art business on Instagram and spending time as a musician and synthesizer player. These experiences have helped me develop creativity, attention to detail, and a practical approach to problem-solving.
