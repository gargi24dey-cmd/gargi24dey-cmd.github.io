About Me

I'm an MSc Economics student at Symbiosis School of Economics, Pune, specializing in Data Analytics — with an academic path that began with a B.Sc. in Economics (Hons) from the University of Calcutta, where I built a foundation in statistics and mathematics alongside economic theory.

What draws me to this field is the ability to turn raw, often messy data into insight that actually informs a decision — whether that's using time-series regression to understand how fuel prices move airline fares, applying econometric techniques to macroeconomic trends across 50 countries, or building interactive dashboards that make socioeconomic data accessible at a glance. I've rounded out these econometric foundations with certifications in social media analytics, applied text mining, and generative AI, since I like being able to bring the right tool to a problem rather than sticking to just one.

Outside of academics, I've run my own small art business on Instagram and spend time as a musician and synthesizer player — pursuits that, in their own way, keep me just as attentive to detail and pattern as my analytical work does.

I'm currently in the final stretch of my dissertation on explainable machine learning for predicting corporate sales slowdowns in Indian listed firms, and I'm always glad to connect with anyone working on similar problems.
