Global Socioeconomic Development Dashboard

An interactive Power BI analysis of global development indicators

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing economic and socioeconomic development across countries using World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) 2022 data. It combines indicators related to economic prosperity, health, digital connectivity, education, and employment to provide a comparative view of development across countries.

Objectives

  • Compare economic and socioeconomic indicators across countries
  • Examine the relationship between GDP per capita and development outcomes
  • Analyze relationships between income, life expectancy, internet usage, education, and unemployment
  • Develop a composite Development Score for cross-country comparison
  • Rank countries according to their overall development performance
  • Allow users to explore the development profile of individual countries

Dataset

The project uses World Bank WDI 2022 data, with key indicators including GDP per capita, life expectancy, internet usage, unemployment rate, and education expenditure. The data was cleaned and transformed using Power Query before analysis and visualization.

Dashboard Structure

Page 1 — Global Development Indicators

An overview using average GDP per capita, life expectancy, internet usage, and unemployment rate, alongside a global GDP per capita map, GDP-vs-life-expectancy analysis, country-level filtering, and key insights.

Page 2 — Economic and Social Relationships

Scatter plots exploring GDP per capita vs. life expectancy, GDP per capita vs. unemployment, GDP per capita vs. internet usage, and education expenditure vs. life expectancy — helping identify patterns and differences across countries.

Page 3 — Development Ranking

Ranks countries using a composite Development Score, alongside the average, highest, and lowest scores, the number of countries analyzed, and key takeaways. The score combines five dimensions:

Development Score = (GDP Score + Life Expectancy Score + Internet Score + Education Score + Employment Score) / 5

This provides a broader measure of development rather than relying only on GDP.

Page 4 — Country Development Profile

An interactive country-level view where users can select a country and examine its Development Score, rank, GDP per capita, life expectancy, internet usage, unemployment rate, education expenditure, individual component scores, and the difference between that country's score and the global average.

Key Insights

  • Countries with higher GDP per capita generally tend to have higher life expectancy
  • Internet usage varies substantially across countries, reflecting differences in digital connectivity
  • Economic prosperity alone does not fully explain socioeconomic development
  • Countries differ considerably in their overall development performance
  • A composite development measure provides a broader perspective than GDP per capita alone
  • Development outcomes reflect multiple dimensions — health, education, employment, and digital access
