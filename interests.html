Interests

Academic Interests

My academic interests are consistently oriented around Indian economic data — including labour markets, financial distress, digital payments, and agricultural economics — using applied quantitative and machine learning methods to explore economic and financial questions relevant to the Indian context.

Career Interests

I'm drawn to roles that sit at the intersection of economics and data — data analytics, econometric research, and business intelligence positions where statistical rigor supports real decision-making. My project work reflects this: from econometric modelling of airline pricing and macroeconomic trends across countries, to building interactive dashboards for socioeconomic data, to applying text mining and generative AI tools to broader analytics problems. I'm particularly interested in opportunities in financial analytics, market research, and data-driven policy or business strategy.
